FAQ

Quick answers.

What is besan?

Besan is gram flour. MAHA AMRUT Besan is made from 100% chana dal.

Which pack sizes are available?

Besan comes in 500 g, 10 kg and 30 kg packs. Vatana Flour is available in a 30 kg bag.

What is Vatana Flour made from?

Vatana Flour is made from 100% vatana dal (yellow peas), as stated on the pack.

Do you supply to businesses?

Yes. We work with retailers, distributors, wholesalers, hotels, caterers and farsan or namkeen makers. Send us an enquiry with your requirement.

How long does it stay fresh?

Best before 3 months from the date of packaging. Store in a cool, dry and hygienic place.

Are prices listed online?

No. Please send an enquiry or message us on WhatsApp for rates and availability.

Where are you based?

Amrut Foods is in Yashodanagar, Indapur, Maharashtra.
